How it works

  • Only $IMD is accepted. Minimum ticket 0.1 IMD.
  • A round lasts 15 minutes from the first ticket. No ticket, no round.
  • After the timer, anyone can finalize. Chainlink VRF supplies the random word; the settlement is atomic in the callback.
  • 70% to the top 3 (40 / 20 / 10), 10% burned, 5% to the treasury, 15% locked in the contract forever.
  • Prizes are pull-based. A multiplier only adds chances to your own tickets, never prize size.
  • If randomness never arrives, anyone can retry after 1 hour, and cancel for refunds after 24 hours.
